## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, institutional atmosphere tailored for enterprise procurement teams, corporate supply chain leads, and industrial vendors. Its personality is analytical, authoritative, and frictionless, stripping away extraneous decoration in favor of structural clarity, predictable spatial rhythm, and high data density.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern B2B SaaS** with **Minimalist Corporate** principles. It relies on crisp low-contrast borders, structured metadata ribbons, explicit status indicators, and clear surface contrast between the canvas and content containers. The interface evokes uncompromised trust, operational speed, and fiscal precision, ensuring mission-critical metrics—such as tenders, deadlines, and multi-currency values—remain legible under demanding corporate workflows.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around deep corporate trust and contextual urgency.

- **Primary (`#1E3A8A` / Blue-900):** Anchors brand presence, key headers, and core interactive priorities in light mode.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` / Blue-600) & Accent (`#3B82F6` / Blue-500):** Drives interactive states, primary CTAs, active pagination, and dark-mode brand anchors.
- **Surface & Canvas:** Canvas layers transition from `#F8FAFC` to `#0B0F19`, while surfaces (cards, sidebars, header) shift from pure `#FFFFFF` to `#1E293B`.
- **Semantic Badging:** Urgency and status tags maintain strict WCAG AAA contrast in both color modes. Dark mode tags utilize tinted alpha backgrounds with complementary 1px perimeter borders to prevent luminous glare on dark canvas surfaces.

## Typography

The type scale is standardized on Inter for superior legibility across multi-column data grids.

- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** All currency amounts (`S/`), item counts, licensing identifiers, and dates must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to guarantee vertical baseline alignment across comparative lists.
- **Visual Weighting:** Titles use semi-bold (`600`) weights to maintain hierarchy without heavy visual clutter. Metric values use bold (`700`) styling with controlled letter-spacing.
- **Compact Scanners:** Labels and metadata tags rely on uppercase or tight title-case variants with deliberate line-height tightening to preserve screen real estate in dense operational views.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform implements an asymmetrical multi-column layout optimized for catalog discovery:

- **Top Navigation Bar:** Sticky top position, spanning full width with inner content constrained to a maximum width of `1440px`. Fixed height of `64px`.
- **Catalog Framework:** 
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Fixed-width left filter drawer/rail (`280px` or `320px`), alongside a 3-column fluid flex/grid results panel using a `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutter.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 2-column results grid; the filter panel transitions into a collapsible vertical accordion or horizontal filter strip.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 1-column card stack; filter sidebar collapses into a sliding bottom-sheet drawer activated by a floating or inline trigger.
- **Rhythm & Metrics:** Internal card padding is locked to `space-lg` (`1.25rem`), metadata chips use `space-xs` vertical with `space-sm` horizontal spacing, and control elements adhere to standard 40px/36px compact form targets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through subtle tonal layering and razor-sharp perimeter definition rather than dramatic shadow spreads.

- **Light Mode Elevation:** Cards and panels sit on pure `#FFFFFF` over the `#F8FAFC` canvas, delimited by a single `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Ambient elevation is applied exclusively on hover via a diffused, low-opacity drop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Dark Mode Elevation:** Depths are defined through surface lightness stepping. Canvas is locked to `#0B0F19`, while floating surfaces advance to `#1E293B`. Outlines use `#334155`. Interactive cards display a subtle border brightening on hover (`#475569`) alongside an internal `1px` translucent inset highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Overlays & Modals:** Modals and slide-out filter drawers use backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over a neutral alpha wash (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`) to preserve spatial context.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined **Soft** shape geometry (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Default Form Controls & Cards (`0.375rem` / `6px`):** Inputs, select boxes, table rows, and requirement cards use an intentional 6px corner radius, balancing modern ergonomics with high enterprise efficiency.
- **Badges & Tags (`0.25rem` / `4px` or Full Pill):** Categorical metadata and status tags adopt crisp 4px corners or compact pill shapes (`9999px`) to distinguish visual taxonomy from clickable surface containers.
- **Dialogs & Menus (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Context menus, popovers, and floating search overlays utilize 8px corner radii for clear elevation bounding.

## Components

### Persistent Header & Brand Bar
- **Brand Cluster:** Left-aligned platform lockup with strong weight font, coupled with a high-contrast badge (`bg-blue-100 text-blue-800 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 font-bold px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-[11px]`).
- **Quick Search:** Centered or inline field with prefix search glyph, tabular shortcut badge (`⌘K`), and muted placeholder (`"Buscar por código, producto o licitación..."`).
- **Theme Switcher:** Compact dual-state segmented control or icon toggle with Sun/Moon icons, executing instant swap of `[data-theme="dark"]` tokens.
- **Primary CTA:** Right-aligned button with leading icon `+`, styled in bold blue (`#2563EB` light / `#3B82F6` dark) with hover brightness transitions.

### Filter Sidebar
- **Header:** Flex row containing `"Filtros"` in `headline-sm` with a right-aligned subtle text button `"Limpiar filtros"`.
- **Inputs & Dropdowns:** Full-width select triggers with trailing chevron indicators; currency range input rendered as twin numeric fields prepended by tabular `S/` currency symbols.
- **Filter Section Separators:** Low-contrast dividers (`#E2E8F0` / `#334155`) spaced at `1.25rem` intervals.

### Requirement Card (Procurement Card)
- **Top Row (Meta Bar):** Monospaced code identifier (`LIC-2026-0042`) in muted slate typography paired with an Urgency Badge (`Alta`, `Media`, or `Baja`) positioned right.
- **Card Body:** Primary requirement title (`headline-md`) clamped to two lines, directly followed by the buyer company name and location pin.
- **Commercial Data Grid:** 2-column or 3-column micro-strip displaying:
  1. *Presupuesto Máximo:* Bold `numeric-metric` with tabular `S/` format.
  2. *Vigencia:* Deadline pill indicator (e.g., `"Vence en 12 días"`).
  3. *Cotizaciones:* Dynamic offer count badge with subtle user/quote icon.
- **Action Strip:** Secondary outline or solid ghost button: `"Ver Ficha Técnica"`, stretching full-width across the base of the card.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Alta:** Light (`bg: #FEE2E2`, `text: #B91C1C`) | Dark (`bg: #7F1D1D40`, `text: #FCA5A5`, `border: 1px solid #DC262650`).
- **Media:** Light (`bg: #FEF3C7`, `text: #B45309`) | Dark (`bg: #78350F40`, `text: #FCD34D`, `border: 1px solid #F59E0B50`).
- **Baja:** Light (`bg: #DCFCE7`, `text: #15803D`) | Dark (`bg: #14532D40`, `text: #86EFAC`, `border: 1px solid #22C55E50`).
- **Status Tags (Abierto / Adjudicado / Cerrado):** Small caps, dot-indicator prefix, using Sky, Purple, and Neutral Slate palettes respectively.

### Pagination Controls
- Clean border-delimited group centered or right-aligned beneath the cards grid: `"Anterior"`, numeric pages `1`, `2`, `3` (with active page indicated by filled secondary blue), and `"Siguiente"`.
